🎯 Why We Chose KNN Based on Recall

In medical diagnosis, recall (sensitivity) measures how well the model identifies actual positive cases. A missed diabetes diagnosis (false negative) is more dangerous than a false alarm (false positive). KNN achieved the highest recall at 61.11%, making it the best choice for minimizing missed diagnoses.

Note: Random Forest achieved higher accuracy (76.62%) but slightly lower recall (59.26%). The choice depends on whether you prioritize overall accuracy or catching all positive cases.

Key Insights

Analytical Findings

📊 Accuracy Analysis

Random Forest leads with 76.62% accuracy, followed by KNN at 75.32%. Decision Tree performed lowest at 68.18%. The ensemble approach of Random Forest helps reduce overfitting.

🎯 Recall Analysis

KNN achieves the highest recall at 61.11%, detecting more actual diabetes cases. For medical screening, this makes KNN the preferred choice as missing a positive case could delay critical treatment.
